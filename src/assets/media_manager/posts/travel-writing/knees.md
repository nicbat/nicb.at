Excerpt from one of my travel writing assignments:\
Worried we bled a little bit onto the stairs, Shaan and I looked at eachother and laughed. We turned our heads around, looking down at our shocked classmates. A bead of sweat rolled down my cheek, we high-fived, and started inching our way down the chalky-white ethereal staircase. Every step stung, sending waves of pain from the bottom up. But it felt so good, we had accomplished something great – something that no one I meet for a long time will be able to say that they did as well. A few sweaty pats on the back later, we made it back down to Earth and were able to laugh with our classmates about the nonsensical thing we had just done. After making it down, along with the rest of our classmates, Shaan and I took our separate paths away from the holy site.
