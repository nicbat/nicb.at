Want to take a moment to document my current (frequently changing) matcha recipes.

### Some notes on everything:

- Water is at 165 degrees (Fahrenheit)
- I'm using a scale accurate to 0.01g - I just use it to weigh my water as well, but of course 1g of water is the same as 1ml
- I would definitely invest in some sort of [chasen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_tea_utensils#Whisks) (the matcha whisks) - when you whisk try to do it in a W or M shape, until there are lots of very small bubbles (I'm sure you can find a great YouTube video)
- I have a very pretty matcha bowl I bought from a very nice family-owned store in Japan

### Usucha (Just matcha powder and water)

This stuff is pretty yummy, but I think you (reader) would only like it if you truly enjoy like plain plain matcha (without sweetness).

- 2g of matcha powder
- 90g (I'm just measuring in grams as well) of water

### Coconut Matcha

Here you'll make the matcha first, then pour the concentrated Usucha on top of the coconut water.

- 2g of matcha powder
- 40g of water
- 8oz of coconut water (I'm just using half of a 16oz bottle of Copra Coconut water)

### Matcha Cold Foam (that I pour on top of coconut water)

> I'm not happy with this recipe yet

This will make two servings. This recipe is a bit more complicated. You'll also need one of those electric milk frothers.

Ingredients:

- 4g of matcha powder
- 35g water
- 25g milk
- 25g heavy cream
- 16oz of coconut water (8oz for each serving)

Steps

- make the super concentrated usucha
- pour in the milk and heavy cream
- froth it for 15-30 seconds - make sure it doesn't get too thick
- pour on top of the coconut water

I'm having trouble making this not super milky. Also I don't like using the frother in my matcha bowl. This recipe will be frequently updated.
